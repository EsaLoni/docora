# Product Context: Docora

## Product
Docora is an AI document intelligence application inspired by Google NotebookLM, engineered for serious reading, synthesis, and grounded knowledge creation.

## Target Users
Students, researchers, analysts, and knowledge workers who read dense technical and academic materials and need verifiable, page-cited answers.

## Core Job
1. **Source Ingestion**: Ingest multi-modal sources (PDFs, Markdown, Web pages, YouTube transcripts, audio).
2. **Grounded Querying**: Ask queries and receive answers grounded strictly in source evidence with interactive page-level citations.
3. **Creative Studio Synthesis**: Generate structured study artifacts (Audio Overviews, Reports, Flashcards, Mind Maps, Quizzes, Slide Decks, and Data Tables).

## Tone & Personality
Calm, focused, precise, trustworthy. Document-first.
- **Feels like**: Linear, Readwise Reader, Notion's reading mode, iA Writer.
- **Does NOT feel like**: A generic AI SaaS template, a chatbot toy, or a hype-filled marketing landing page.

## Technical Constraints
- Next.js 16, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS.
- Frontend UI/UX only; strict preservation of existing backend API contracts, auth, and state machines.
